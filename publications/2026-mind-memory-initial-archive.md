## Core Idea

This is the initial versioned release (v0.1.0) of the *Ferdi Iskandar Mind & Memory* repository — a structured personal knowledge base that captures a way of thinking, decision-making process, lessons learned, vision, and values across leadership, healthcare, artificial intelligence, governance, innovation, and human-centered system building.

The Zenodo release creates a permanent, citable, DOI-referenced snapshot of the repository at its initial structural state — making the knowledge archive itself part of the open scientific record.

## Why This Matters

The act of archiving a personal knowledge repository on Zenodo — and assigning it a DOI — is itself a statement: that organized personal thinking, structured intellectual memory, and documented decision-making are valuable artifacts worthy of preservation and citation.

In a research and practice culture that often privileges formal journal publications over the thinking processes that generate them, this archive asserts that the *infrastructure of thought* is as important as its outputs.
